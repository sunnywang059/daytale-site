DayTale Support

DayTale turns your Apple Watch into an AI recorder. Recordings are transcribed on your iPhone.

Contact

Email sunnywang059@gmail.com. Please do not include API keys, sign-in credentials, or recordings of people who have not agreed to share them.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Recording from the wrist needs an Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later and the DayTale watch app.

Common questions

A recording has not reached my iPhone. Keep the iPhone nearby and unlocked for a moment, then open DayTale on the watch or the iPhone. Audio stays on the watch until the iPhone confirms it has a complete copy.

Is anything uploaded? Not unless you send it. Cloud summaries and cloud transcription are optional, use a service you configure, and show what will be sent before anything leaves your phone.

How do I delete data? Swipe a recording to delete its audio or the whole record, or use Settings to clear content.

よくある質問

録音がiPhoneに届きません。 iPhoneを近くに置いてロックを解除し、WatchまたはiPhoneでDayTaleを開いてください。iPhoneが完全なコピーを受け取るまで、音声はWatchに残ります。

何かアップロードされますか？ 自分で送信しない限り、何もアップロードされません。クラウド要約とクラウド文字起こしは任意で、ご自身で設定したサービスを使い、送信前に内容を確認できます。

データを削除するには？ 録音をスワイプして音声または記録全体を削除するか、「設定」から内容を消去できます。
